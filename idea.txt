# How The System Detects Active Subscriptions

This is the important technical logic.

The AI identifies recurring debits by analyzing:

* merchant/payment destination
* narration/transaction description
* frequency patterns
* recurring amount patterns
* merchant category

Example:

| Transaction Narration | Frequency | AI Interpretation       |
| --------------------- | --------- | ----------------------- |
| NETFLIX.COM           | Monthly   | Netflix subscription    |
| Spotify NG            | Monthly   | Spotify Premium         |
| GOtv Renewal          | Monthly   | TV subscription         |
| Apple Services        | Monthly   | iCloud/Apple Music      |
| ChatGPT Subscription  | Monthly   | AI service subscription |

# Subscription Detection Logic

You can explain it like this:

## Step 1 — Transaction Ingestion

The system collects:

* debit transactions
* merchant names
* timestamps
* narration strings
* amount
* payment channel

---

## Step 2 — Recurring Pattern Analysis

The AI checks:

* repeated merchants
* repeated time intervals
* near-identical amounts
* recurring billing windows

Example:

* ₦7,500 every 30 days to Netflix
* ₦6,200 every month to Spotify

→ classified as subscription.

## Step 3 — Subscription Classification

The AI categorizes subscriptions:
